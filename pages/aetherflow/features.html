  • • Calculate remaining operational time
  • • Monitor duration thresholds
  • • Flag equipment approaching failure
  • • Predict which components will fail

  • • Reduce customer service disruptions
  • • Optimize maintenance scheduling
  • • Lower overall maintenance costs
